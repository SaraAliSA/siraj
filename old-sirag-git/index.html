🎯 Problem & Solution

⚠️
The Problem
  • Users have banking data but don't understand their financial situation
  • No insight into spending habits or patterns
  • Unable to prepare for seasonal expenses (Ramadan, Eid, vacations)
  • Can't detect unnecessary recurring subscriptions
  • Current banking apps display data but rarely explain what users should do
  • No culturally-aware financial guidance in Arabic
✅
Our Solution
  • AI continuously analyzes financial behavior and predicts outcomes
  • Natural language explanations instead of just numbers and charts
  • Proactive seasonal planning for Ramadan, Eid, and school season
  • Smart alerts with evidence-based explanations (the WHY)
  • AI Financial Coach that simulates outcomes before answering
  • Islamic finance principles and Saudi dialect Arabic

🤖 Core AI Features

📊
AI Financial Health Score
Intelligent score with explanations of why it changed, positive/negative behaviors, and predicted next month.
🌅
AI Financial Briefing
Dashboard opens with an AI-written summary instead of charts. Personalized, warm, and actionable.
📈
AI Spending Analysis
Analyzes categories, income, cash flow, and trends — explained in natural language, not just numbers.
💬
AI Financial Coach
Ask questions like "Can I afford a new car?" — the AI simulates outcomes before answering.
🔮
AI Scenario Simulator
Simulate buying a car, salary changes, new loans, and see future cash flow and budget impact.
📅
AI Seasonal Planner
Auto-prepare for Ramadan, Eid, summer vacation with predicted expenses and savings plans.
🔔
AI Smart Alerts
Proactive alerts like "You may exceed dining budget Thursday" — each alert explains WHY.
📑
AI Subscription Manager
Auto-detects recurring subscriptions, projects 12/24-month costs, and suggests evidence-based reviews.
🧠
AI Habit Detection
Detects weekend overspending, coffee habits, delivery dependency, and recommends improvements.
📝
AI Monthly Review
Personalized monthly report with achievements, risks, improvements, and predictions in natural language.

🇸🇦 Target Market

🎯
Saudi Arabia — Islamic Finance Alignment
  • Respects Islamic finance principles — avoids riba (interest), budgets for Zakat
  • Warm Saudi dialect Arabic — conversational, not robotic
  • Cultural awareness — Ramadan spending, Eid preparations, wedding planning
  • Understands Saudi lifestyle — car costs, electricity bills, delivery culture
  • Family-centric — supports joint finances and family obligations
